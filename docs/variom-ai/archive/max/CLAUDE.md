## Non-negotiable execution rules
- The approved product architecture is the consolidated record of user-approved product decisions. Do not silently contradict or narrow it.
- The active task brief limits what may be changed; these instructions do not authorize work beyond that brief.
- The project may remain in specification/audit only. Do not implement application features until the user explicitly authorizes implementation after information gathering and design are complete.
- Apply Maximum Quality, Minimum Overhead. Avoid scope creep, unnecessary agents/Skills/services/dependencies, redundant model calls, and rerunning unaffected work.
- No monthly content calendar or publishing-planning feature.
- Simple Smart Editing has no preview stage before export and is not a professional timeline editor.
- Verify provider/API capabilities and current prices; never assume them.
- Do not invent product facts, provider capabilities, prices, test outcomes, or completion evidence.
- Preserve the creative library's existing taxonomy, schemas, records, references, and draft/approved states unless the active task explicitly authorizes a scoped change.
- Inspect the live repository, branch, uncommitted changes, and relevant tests before editing.
- Update the Markdown tracker with verified facts. Never mark a task accepted without review.
