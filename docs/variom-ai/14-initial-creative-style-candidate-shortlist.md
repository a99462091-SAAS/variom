# VARIOM — Initial Creative Style Candidate Shortlist

**Date:** 2026-11-19  
**Status:** Initial shortlist for manual review and provider validation — not production approval.  
**Authority:** Subordinate to `00-product-vision-simplified-ux-v2.md` and the owner-approved policy in `13-owner-decisions-creative-taxonomy.md`.

## 1. Purpose and selection approach

This document narrows the 144 MAX source records to a deliberately small first test set. It is intended to reduce review and validation effort while covering distinct user needs across the eight VARIOM categories.

Selection considered:
- Direct usefulness for a likely VARIOM user task.
- A clear creative purpose that is distinguishable from nearby candidates.
- A manageable initial brief and required inputs.
- Avoidance of unresolved IDs, unsupported factual claims, rights assumptions and unverified advanced production.
- Fit with the authoritative VARIOM category, not the old MAX category label alone.

This is a qualitative shortlist, not a performance ranking. The MAX source records remain `review`; image suitability/cost labels are only source estimates, and all video suitability/cost/complexity values remain unknown. No provider capability or output quality is established here.

## 3. Deliberately excluded from this first test set

These records are not necessarily rejected; they are deferred to avoid unnecessary complexity or unresolved product questions.

- **P1:** defer P1-012 scale/size and P1-014 ingredients/components until verified dimensions and composition data are available; defer P1-018 compositing until product-fidelity capability is proven.
- **P2:** defer price/discount, comparison, evidence, testimonial, urgency and guarantee formats until the required current facts, evidence, terms and permissions are available. P2-004, P2-006, P2-009, P2-010, P2-012 and P2-013 are examples.
- **P3:** P3-013…P3-018 remain held pending canonical ID/name reconciliation. Other non-shortlisted styles can be considered after the first two patterns are tested. Carousel remains Image Studio only.
- **P4:** defer before/after, professional, cultural and impact claims until source facts, consent and human review are available.
- **V1:** defer real testimonial, first-person real experience, reaction and expert formats until provenance, identity, consent and disclosure are verifiable. V1-010 and V1-015 are selected specifically to avoid presenting synthetic or acted content as real customer testimony.
- **V2:** defer installation, operation, before/after, comparison, dimensions and planned camera movement until factual instructions and provider capability are validated. V2-016 voice-over and V2-017 annotation remain possible production modifiers, not separately approved styles.
- **V3:** defer 3D, product visualization, simulation, complex concept and advanced hybrid motion until a working production path is demonstrated.
- **V4:** V4-008 is a possible alternative story candidate but is deferred until its distinction from V4-011 is demonstrated. V4-017 is a multi-scene structure, not automatically a story style. No MAX V4 entry is accepted as a testimonial by category label.

## 4. Validation sequence

Run the validation in two small waves to avoid testing every candidate at once.

### Wave A — Image candidate feasibility

Test the eight image candidates with representative, owner-approved brand/product inputs:
- Verify product identity, color, labels and proportions against the supplied source image.
- Verify Arabic and English copy handling, especially headline layout.
- Verify category distinction: P1 product presentation, P2 ad message, P3 slide sequence, P4 real context.
- Record output quality, failure/repair count, generation time, human correction effort and actual provider cost.
- Review all factual claims, product details, rights and human representation.

### Wave B — Video candidate feasibility

Test the seven video candidates only after confirming a real provider/tool path:
- Use VARIOM-supported durations only: 10, 20 or 30 seconds; do not infer support for all durations from a single test.
- Verify product/character continuity, motion, scene order, voice/text synchronization and actual output files.
- Test Arabic and English, including Arabic text composition and RTL.
- For V1, label acted or synthetic performances honestly; do not fabricate real experiences or reactions.
- For V4-011, validate an actual coherent story arc; do not treat an offer-led commercial or a sequence of unrelated shots as a story.
- Record actual success/failure and repair rates, generation time, human effort, and provider cost.

### Acceptance rule

A candidate advances only if:
1. Its purpose is distinct and useful within the VARIOM category.
2. Required facts, assets, rights and permissions are available.
3. The intended provider/tool produces a usable result under the actual supported settings.
4. Product, brand, language and identity checks pass.
5. Cost, failure/repair rate and human effort are acceptable for the product.
6. The owner accepts the final VARIOM-authored record.

A failed candidate remains deferred or is removed; it is not retained merely to fill a category.

## 5. Important boundaries

- This shortlist does not change the source JSON or application data.
- It does not approve any source record, prompt, reference image, provider or generated result.
- It does not authorize schema, API, database, UI or workflow changes.
- It does not grant rights to reuse linked MAX reference media.
- It does not make claims about conversion, sales performance or provider capability.
- The final VARIOM style records must be rewritten to the approved VARIOM schema, with stable VARIOM IDs and explicit historical aliases where appropriate.
