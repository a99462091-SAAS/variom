# VARIOM — Real-World Content Simulation

**Status:** Active  
**Purpose:** Document a practical, owner-led simulation of VARIOM's brand intelligence, monthly content ideation, copywriting, and production-prompt workflows across three real social-media brands.  
**Repository:** `a99462091-SAAS/variom`  
**Isolation:** This is a test/evidence sidecar. It does not amend Product Vision V2.0, approve product scope, or represent unfinished capabilities as implemented.

## Simulation protocol

1. Complete a separate Brand DNA record for each brand.
2. Configure the monthly content request for that brand.
3. Generate and review the content strategy and ideas.
4. Record owner edits, approvals, and rejections with reasons.
5. Produce one production prompt at a time after idea approval.
6. Record Gemini outputs and evaluate their alignment with the prompt, brand, and marketing objective.
7. Keep the three brands' identities, assets, language rules, and evaluations separate.

## Brand DNA discovery — approved simulation template

**Owner-approved approach (2026-11-24):** Use a compact, adaptive discovery flow, not a broad company questionnaire. Do not ask again for information already available in registration or the brand's existing profile. Ask only for missing information that materially affects brand understanding and content quality. Questions may be skipped when the answer is already known or unavailable.

### Existing profile data — reuse, do not re-ask

- Brand name
- Business category
- Target audience
- Logo, if supplied

### Core discovery questions

1. **Differentiation and value:** What makes the brand different from competitors, and what core value does it offer?
2. **Audience understanding:** What are the audience's main needs/problems, and what motivates them to choose this brand?
3. **Brand personality and communication:** What personality should the brand express? What language/dialect and communication style should it use?
4. **Visual identity:** What are the available brand colors, fonts, image/design style, and visual references? Ask only for missing or unclear elements.
5. **Products and services:** What are the relevant products/services, their key benefits, and any important marketing facts? Reuse supplied information and do not invent claims.

### Discovery rules

- Keep questions concise and adaptive; do not force every question when the information is already available.
- Do not expand discovery into a generic audit of every business function.
- Mark unknowns as **NOT PROVIDED / NOT VERIFIED** rather than guessing.
- Separate owner-provided facts from assistant interpretations and proposals.
- Present the resulting Brand DNA summary for owner review and approval before using it as the basis for the monthly content cycle.

## Evaluation rubric

Score each applicable dimension from 1–5 and include evidence:

1. Brand-DNA fidelity
2. Strategic fit with the monthly objective
3. Audience relevance and insight
4. Idea originality and usefulness
5. Copywriting quality and clarity
6. Arabic/English and dialect correctness, where applicable
7. Visual-direction and typography specificity
8. Prompt completeness, precision, and controllability
9. Gemini output alignment with the approved prompt
10. Overall readiness for publication (assessment only; publishing is outside this simulation)

Do not fabricate metrics or claim campaign performance without real performance data. Distinguish prompt quality from external generator quality.
