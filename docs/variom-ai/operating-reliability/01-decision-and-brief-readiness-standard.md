# VARIOM — Task Readiness & Creative Brief Control

**Document ID:** V-OR-01  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** OWNER-APPROVED  
**Authority:** Master Strategy V1.2 and the six approved knowledge documents remain controlling.  
**Purpose:** Operational control for task readiness; not a separate customer workflow.

## 1. Scope and rule

Apply this control only to the task at hand. Internal strategy selection and creative decisions remain VARIOM responsibilities; customers provide objectives, priorities, and approvals—not internal strategy names or routine technical decisions.

This standard does not amend the master strategy or approved knowledge libraries. If a conflict appears, record it and seek owner decision; do not silently reconcile it.

## 2. Readiness checks

Before production-route assessment, the task record must establish, proportionate to task complexity:

- Task identity, requested deliverable, and approved objective/plan/concept reference.
- Relevant brand context, audience, market, language, channel, and format when known or material.
- Applicable knowledge sources and their approved versions/statuses. Apply only relevant libraries; do not force irrelevant ones.
- Core message, copy requirements, creative direction, and task-specific acceptance criteria.
- Material facts/claims with evidence source and disposition.
- Essential assets and any fidelity or usage restrictions.
- Known limitations, dependencies, and approval boundaries.

Use existing approved context; do not ask the customer to repeat available information. Missing non-critical information may be omitted or handled as a clearly recorded safe assumption. Do not invent facts, assets, claims, prices, offers, compatibility, certifications, testimonials, warranties, or performance results.

## 3. Claim and uncertainty handling

For each material claim, record one concise evidence state: **VERIFIED**, **CUSTOMER-PROVIDED**, **UNVERIFIED**, **ASSUMPTION**, or **OMITTED**, with source and decision where relevant. Customer-provided claims are not automatically independently verified. Claims involving compatibility, performance, certification, warranty, pricing, offers, scarcity, customer results, or comparative superiority require appropriate substantiation; exclude, neutralize, or block when necessary.

## 4. Task readiness outcome

- **READY_FOR_ROUTE_ASSESSMENT:** required information is sufficiently resolved; proceed to R2 only.
- **READY_WITH_LIMITATIONS:** bounded non-critical omissions are recorded and safe.
- **BLOCKED:** critical requirement, fact, asset, or feasibility issue is unresolved.
- **OWNER_DECISION_REQUIRED:** material conflict, exception, or approval requires owner authority.

Readiness never authorizes production, spending, final acceptance, or publication.

## 5. Stop conditions

Block or escalate if the task would conflict with approved direction; requires unsupported material claims; lacks an essential authentic asset with no safe alternative; requires fabrication of brand/product evidence; has unresolved material source conflicts; or requires an approval not granted. Do not block for safe-to-omit non-critical details.

## 6. Record and acceptance

Maintain this evidence in the **single Unified Task Execution Record** (defined in the Operating Reliability README), not in a duplicate R1 report. R1 passes only when approved direction, applicable knowledge, claims, essential assets, constraints, readiness rationale, and acceptance criteria are traceable and the status is supported.

## 7. Negative cases

Validate at least the existing R1-N01–N10 scenarios: unsupported compatibility; missing essential authentic asset; unknown official font; changed approved objective; irrelevant library; conflicting sources; complete-looking brief with unresolved critical issue; approval without spending authorization; context already available; and safe omission of a non-critical detail.

## 8. Governance

OWNER-APPROVED as an operational standard. Platform implementation still requires separate explicit authorization.

**Operating rule:** A polished prompt is not readiness evidence. Advance only when the approved direction, material facts, assets, constraints, and task acceptance criteria are sufficiently resolved. R1 is not execution authorization.
