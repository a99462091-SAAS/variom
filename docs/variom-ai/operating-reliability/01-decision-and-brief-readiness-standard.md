# VARIOM — Decision & Creative Brief Readiness Standard

**Document ID:** V-OR-01  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** DRAFT — OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED  
**Document type:** Operational companion standard  
**Parent authority:** VARIOM — Intelligent Advertising Strategy & Operating Framework, Version 1.2  
**Scope:** R1 — Decision and Brief Readiness

---

## 1. Purpose

This standard makes the existing requirements of the approved master strategy and the six approved knowledge documents operationally checkable before a creative task is handed to production.

It does not replace, amend, or outrank the master strategy or any approved knowledge document. If a conflict is found, the approved source document remains authoritative and the conflict must be recorded for owner review rather than silently resolved.

The standard is intended to prevent incomplete, inconsistent, factually unsafe, or infeasible creative briefs from advancing to production.

## 2. Governing principles

1. Apply only the knowledge components relevant to the requested task; do not force every library into every task.
2. Internal selection and coordination remain VARIOM responsibilities. Do not turn internal decisions into customer questionnaires.
3. Preserve approved objectives, marketing direction, material concepts, and messages.
4. Separate verified facts, customer-provided information, provider-declared capabilities, observed results, assumptions, and recommendations.
5. Do not invent missing brand assets, product details, compatibility, proof, prices, offers, certifications, testimonials, warranty terms, or performance claims.
6. Missing non-essential information may be handled through a safe, explicitly recorded assumption or omission. Missing critical information blocks only the affected task or element.
7. Brief depth must be proportionate to task complexity and risk.
8. A brief marked ready is not evidence that production has occurred, that a provider is suitable, that spending is authorized, or that the final content is accepted.

## 3. Scope and authority of the six knowledge documents

| Source | Required contribution when relevant | It must not be used to |
|---|---|---|
| Master Strategy V1.2 | Define the four-stage operating flow, approval boundaries, quality principles, and Stage 3 brief output | Treat a brief or simulation as platform implementation |
| V-KD-01 Content Marketing Strategies Library | Validate marketing-strategy relevance to the approved objective and audience | Expose internal strategy selection as a customer configuration task |

The applicable source versions and statuses must be recorded. Knowledge that is not eligible under its governance status must not be represented as approved guidance.

## 4. R1 readiness workflow

R1 has five internal operations. They are not customer-facing checkpoints.

### R1.1 Resolve the task context

Capture only information already supplied, approved, or reliably available:

- Content/task identifier and requested output.
- Approved brand context and applicable restrictions.
- Approved marketing objective and plan reference, where applicable.
- Approved idea or concept, if already selected.
- Intended audience, market, language, channel, and format when known.
- Customer approvals relevant to this task and their exact scope.
- Required assets and known source locations.

Do not ask the customer to repeat information already available. Do not infer a missing fact merely because it would make the brief easier to write.

### R1.2 Apply relevant knowledge

For each relevant knowledge document, record one of:

- **APPLIED:** a rule or selection materially informed this task.
- **NOT_APPLICABLE:** the document's scope is not relevant to this task.
- **BLOCKED:** relevant guidance could not be applied because a required input or eligible knowledge entry is missing.
- **CONFLICT:** applicable guidance appears inconsistent; escalate rather than silently choose.

A simple task may legitimately use fewer than all six libraries. The record must explain non-applicability only where the omission could otherwise be mistaken for an overlooked requirement.

### R1.3 Resolve facts, claims, and uncertainty

Every material factual statement in the proposed content must have a traceable basis, such as approved Brand DNA, a customer-supplied source, a verified product source, or another reliable approved record.

Assign one evidence state to each material claim:

- **VERIFIED:** supported by an identified reliable source.
- **CUSTOMER-PROVIDED:** supplied by the customer but not independently verified; use only where the brand's approval and risk permit.
- **UNVERIFIED:** lacks adequate support; exclude, rewrite neutrally, or block the affected content.
- **ASSUMPTION:** a limited working assumption, clearly marked and not presented as a fact.
- **OMITTED:** intentionally excluded because evidence is absent or the claim is unnecessary.

Claims involving product performance, compatibility, certifications, warranty, price, offers, scarcity, customer results, or comparative superiority require appropriate evidence. A general brand positioning statement must not be expanded into a specific measurable promise without support.

### R1.4 Produce the task-appropriate Creative Production Brief

The brief must contain the applicable fields below. Fields that do not apply may be omitted; blank fields must not be disguised as completed decisions.

**Core fields**
- Task ID, version, and status.
- Approved objective and plan/concept reference.
- Audience, market, language, channel, and output format when known.
- Core message and approved copy, or copy requirements.
- Creative category, format, style, treatment, and rationale as relevant.
- Composition, scene, or shot direction as relevant.
- Required assets, source references, and protected elements.
- Elements that may be generated, edited, or composited.
- Brand, factual, legal, and technical restrictions.
- Deliverable specifications.
- Task-specific acceptance criteria.
- Known uncertainties, exclusions, dependencies, and unresolved risks.
- Applicable customer approval and authorization references.

**Proportionality rule:** Do not force irrelevant fields into a simple task. A static text-led design does not require a video shot plan. A video task may require scene continuity, timing, audio, and shot requirements. The brief is complete when all material requirements for the actual task are resolved—not when every possible field is populated.

### R1.5 Run the readiness decision

The brief receives exactly one readiness status:

- **READY_FOR_PRODUCTION_REVIEW:** R1 requirements are satisfied; the task may proceed to the separate production-method and asset-readiness assessment. This is not permission to execute.
- **READY_WITH_RECORDED_LIMITATIONS:** the task can proceed to the next assessment with explicitly bounded non-critical omissions that do not compromise truth, brand integrity, or required output.
- **BLOCKED:** one or more critical requirements are unresolved.
- **REQUIRES_OWNER_DECISION:** a material conflict, exception, or approval boundary requires owner authority.

R1 must never set a paid-execution, final-acceptance, or publication status.

## 5. Mandatory stop conditions

Set **BLOCKED** or **REQUIRES_OWNER_DECISION** when any of the following affects the requested deliverable:

1. The task conflicts with an approved objective, material concept, or brand restriction.
2. A material product or performance claim has no adequate evidence and cannot be safely omitted or rewritten.
3. An essential asset is missing and no truthful, acceptable alternative exists.
4. The requested representation would require fabricating a logo, package, product model, certification, testimonial, or other brand evidence.
5. Required language, format, or technical constraints cannot be stated clearly enough to evaluate feasibility.
6. A material conflict exists between applicable approved knowledge sources.
7. The task requires an approval that has not been granted.
8. The brief would materially change an approved message or concept without the required approval.

A non-critical missing detail must not automatically block the entire task if it can be omitted or handled safely without misleading the audience or degrading a required outcome.

## 6. R1 evidence record

Keep one concise, versioned record per content item. At minimum:

| Field | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Task identity | Content ID, brief version, date/status |
| Approved direction | Objective, plan, concept, and approval references |
| Knowledge application | Applicable document IDs/versions and APPLIED / NOT_APPLICABLE / BLOCKED / CONFLICT state |
| Claims and facts | Material claim, evidence state, source reference, disposition |
| Assets | Required asset, source/reference, availability, protection requirement |
| Constraints | Relevant brand, language, format, and technical restrictions |
| Decisions | Material selection, concise rationale, and decision owner/source |
| Readiness | Status, blocking reasons, limitations, and next required gate |

Do not create duplicate reports for each library. Do not record verbose reasoning that does not improve traceability, safety, or future diagnosis.

## 7. R1 acceptance criteria

R1 is considered passed for a task only when all applicable criteria are evidenced:

1. Approved direction is identified and has not been silently changed.
2. Relevant knowledge documents were applied according to scope and eligibility.
3. Non-applicable knowledge was not forced into the task.
4. Material claims have evidence states and unsafe claims are excluded or blocked.
5. Essential assets and protected elements are identified.
6. The brief is sufficiently complete for the specific task and proportionate in detail.
7. Missing information is handled safely, without inventing facts or burdening the customer with avoidable questions.
8. Material conflicts and approval dependencies are surfaced.
9. Task-specific acceptance criteria are stated.
10. The readiness status is consistent with the evidence record.
11. No production, spending, final acceptance, or publication authorization is implied.

## 8. Required negative tests

The following cases must be tested before this standard is considered operationally validated:

| Test | Input condition | Expected R1 result |
|---|---|---|
| R1-N01 | Product compatibility claim without model/source evidence | Claim excluded or task blocked if essential |
| R1-N02 | Missing essential authentic product image | Block affected product-hero task; do not invent an image |
| R1-N03 | Missing official font specification | Do not invent a brand font; choose a safe treatment or record limitation |
| R1-N04 | Approved objective changed by a creative suggestion | Require the applicable approval before changing direction |
| R1-N05 | One library is irrelevant to a simple task | Mark not applicable; do not fabricate application |
| R1-N06 | Two applicable approved sources materially conflict | Requires owner decision; do not silently reconcile |
| R1-N07 | Brief contains all headings but unresolved critical claims | Blocked, not ready |
| R1-N08 | Customer approval exists but financial authorization does not | R1 may pass; paid execution remains prohibited by the master strategy |
| R1-N09 | Customer information is already available in approved context | Reuse it; do not ask the customer to repeat it |
| R1-N10 | Non-critical detail is missing and safely omissible | Proceed with recorded limitation where appropriate |

---

## Final operating rule

**No creative task advances from decision-making to production-method assessment on the strength of a polished prompt alone. It advances only when its approved direction, applicable knowledge, factual basis, essential assets, constraints, and task-specific acceptance criteria are sufficiently resolved and traceable.**

**R1 readiness is not production authorization.**
